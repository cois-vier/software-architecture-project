Assignment 1: Conceptual Architecture

We recovered WezTerm's conceptual architecture from its official documentation. We describe it as four layers (presentation, session, terminal and system) built around a multiplexer and its domains, combined with a client–server relationship and implicit invocation through Lua event handlers.
